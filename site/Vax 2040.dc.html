A message from our founder

Africa carries a quarter of the world's disease burden and manufactures almost none of the medicine it needs. When supply lines closed during the last pandemic, that arithmetic stopped being a statistic and became a matter of survival.

Vax 2040 was founded on a simple conviction: sovereignty in health cannot be donated, it has to be built — and it has to be measured. We are not a manufacturer and not a lobby. We are an independent research mechanism that counts what is actually being produced, where capability is thin, and what stands between this continent and the African Union's 2040 target.

Our work is offered freely to the governments, regulators, manufacturers and journalists who need it. Thank you for taking an interest in it.

Moses Bagarirayose
KIGALI, RWANDA

About us

WHO WE ARE

Vax 2040 is a philanthropic research and development entity dedicated to deepening career and capability in the pharmaceutical manufacturing of vaccines and other health commodities.

Our geography is the Eastern and Central regions of Africa and beyond — wherever the manufacturing of vaccines and pharmaceuticals expresses a great need.

We are independent by design. We do not manufacture, sell or procure. Our sole output is evidence: an open, data-driven account of where African pharmaceutical manufacturing actually stands against the African Union's 2040 target, and what has to change for that target to be met.

That evidence is built for the people who can act on it — health ministries, national regulators, manufacturers, development financiers and the journalists who hold all of them to account.

AREAS, METHODOLOGY & INDICATORS

Research

Focused research areas driving pharmaceutical development in Africa, and the framework we use to measure them.

01
Manufacturing · Infrastructure

Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Growth Index

A comprehensive analysis of pharmaceutical manufacturing capacity, infrastructure and investment across Africa — plant by plant, product by product — so that progress toward the 2040 target can be stated as a number rather than an aspiration.

02
Supply Chain · Raw Materials

Raw Materials Security Surveillance

Monitoring and securing the supply of active ingredients, excipients and packaging on which local production depends. Manufacturing sovereignty is only real if the inputs are not a single shock away from stopping.

03
Procurement · Regulation

Pooled Procurement and Market Regulation

Strengthening collective purchasing mechanisms and the regulatory frameworks around them, so that African-made medicines reach a market large enough and predictable enough to justify the factory that makes them.

04
Therapeutics · Diagnostics

Accelerate the Next Generation of Therapy and Diagnostics

Tracking and advocating for African participation in advanced therapeutics, precision medicine and diagnostic capability — the technologies that will define the next two decades of the field, not the last two.
