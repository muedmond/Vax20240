Welcome

Africa should not have to ask permission to make its own medicine.

Welcome to the African Pharmaceutical Sovereignty Assessment Project. Our aim is straightforward: to help Africa move from importing most of its medicines and vaccines to making them here. The continent depends on supply chains it does not control, and every global disruption shows how exposed that leaves us.

We measure progress against a single target — 60% of the medicines and vaccines used in Africa manufactured in Africa by 2040. We track the whole value chain, not just finished products, including whether the continent can make its own active pharmaceutical ingredients and secure a steady supply of raw materials.

Manufacturing capacity is only half the problem. We also monitor how governments buy medicines and what they budget for them, asking whether procurement rules genuinely favor African-made products and whether national spending matches the urgency of the goal. Where policy stands in the way, we say so.

We ensure that strategic long-term planning matches the financial investments required to build a resilient, competitive, and highly regulated continental market.

Data on its own changes nothing. Every assessment therefore ends with practical recommendations that institutions can act on — clear steps for governments, regional bodies and manufacturers to raise local production. I invite you to read our findings, question them, and work with us.

Moise Bagarirayose Kigali, Rwanda

About the Center

Evidence built for the people who can act on it

An independent research mechanism measuring where African pharmaceutical manufacturing actually stands against the 2040 target.

Who we are

Vax 2040 is a philanthropic research and development entity dedicated to deepening capability in the pharmaceutical manufacturing of vaccines and other health commodities.

Our geography is the Eastern and Central regions of Africa and beyond — wherever the manufacturing of vaccines and pharmaceuticals expresses a great need.

We are independent by design. We do not manufacture, sell or procure. Our sole output is evidence: an open, data-driven account of where African pharmaceutical manufacturing actually stands against the African Union's 2040 target, and what has to change for that target to be met.

That evidence is built for the people who can act on it — health ministries, national regulators, manufacturers, development financiers and the journalists who hold all of them to account.

Who leads the work

Mr Moise Bagarirayose

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Moise Bagarirayose is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of Vax 2040. A pharmacist by training, he holds a master's degree in Pharmacy and brings more than fifteen years of professional experience in the pharmaceutical and health sector to the Center's work.

He leads the Center's research agenda — the Manufacturing Growth Index, raw-material security surveillance, pooled procurement and next-generation therapeutics — and represents Vax 2040 in its engagement with regulators, manufacturers and philanthropic partners across the region.
